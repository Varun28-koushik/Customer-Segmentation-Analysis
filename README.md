## Customer Segmentation Analysis

Segmented 200 mall customers (Mall Customers dataset - Kaggle) 
across age, annual income, and spending score to identify distinct 
customer groups and inform marketing strategy.

## Tools Used
Excel, Pivot Tables, Customer Analytics

## Segments Identified
- High Income, High Spend — prime loyalty programme targets
- High Income, Low Spend — re-engagement opportunity
- Low Income, High Spend — price-sensitive loyalists
- Average Income, Average Spend — largest segment by volume

## Key Finding
Adults aged 25-40 were the highest-spending cohort. The High Value 
segment contributed disproportionately to revenue despite being the 
smallest group by headcount.

## Skills Demonstrated
Customer segmentation, data modeling, statistical analysis, 
marketing analytics, actionable business recommendations
